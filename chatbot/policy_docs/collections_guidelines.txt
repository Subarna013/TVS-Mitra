EMI COLLECTIONS GUIDELINES – TVS CREDIT

TVS Credit follows responsible, transparent, and customer-friendly collection practices in line with applicable regulations and internal policies.

COLLECTION COMMUNICATION PRACTICES
• EMI reminder calls or messages are made only during permitted hours.
• Communications are intended to remind customers of upcoming or due EMIs.
• Calls or messages are polite, non-threatening, and informative in nature.

CUSTOMER RESPECT AND PRIVACY
• TVS Credit respects customer dignity and privacy at all times.
• No abusive, aggressive, or intimidating language is used in any communication.
• Customer information is kept confidential and shared only through authorized channels.

PAYMENT CONFIRMATION AND DISPUTES
• Customers are not required to make duplicate payments if they have already paid.
• In case of payment disputes, customers may be asked to share transaction references for verification.
• Any mismatch between payment and system status is handled through reconciliation processes.

COLLECTION TIMINGS
• EMI-related calls and messages are sent during reasonable hours as per policy.
• No repeated or excessive calls are made within short time intervals.

CUSTOMER RIGHTS
• Customers have the right to seek clarification on EMI amounts, due dates, and payment status.
• Customers may request assistance or escalation through official support channels.
• Customers are encouraged to report any suspicious or unauthorized communication.

NO COERCIVE ACTIONS
• TVS Credit does not use threats, pressure, or coercion to recover payments.
• Legal action, if applicable, follows due process and is not communicated through informal channels.

PAYMENT CHANNEL SAFETY
• Payments should only be made through official TVS Credit payment channels.
• TVS Credit does not request sensitive information such as OTPs, passwords, or card details via calls or messages.

ESCALATION AND SUPPORT
• For unresolved EMI-related issues, customers can contact TVS Credit customer support.
• Support teams assist with payment clarification, disputes, and status updates.

These guidelines ensure a fair, transparent, and respectful EMI collection experience for all customers.
