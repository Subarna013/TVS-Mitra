EMI POLICY – TVS CREDIT

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount that a customer agrees to pay every month towards repayment of a loan taken from TVS Credit. The EMI consists of both principal and interest components.

Each EMI has a defined due date mentioned in the loan agreement. Customers are expected to ensure sufficient balance in their bank account or selected payment mode on or before the due date.

If an EMI is paid on or before the due date, the payment is considered on-time and no additional charges apply.

If an EMI is not paid by the due date, it is treated as overdue. Overdue EMIs may attract late payment charges or penalty interest as per the loan agreement terms.

In some cases, there may be a short grace period after the due date during which payment can still be made without major impact. However, the availability and duration of any grace period depend on the specific loan product and agreement.

If an EMI remains unpaid beyond the allowed period, reminder notifications may be sent through calls, SMS, WhatsApp, or other communication channels to inform the customer about the pending payment.

Repeated non-payment of EMIs can impact the customer’s credit score and may be reported to credit bureaus as per regulatory guidelines.

TVS Credit may initiate structured reminder or collections processes for overdue EMIs. These communications are intended to inform customers and help them regularize their payments in a timely manner.

Customers who have already paid their EMI but continue to receive reminders may experience this due to processing delays, bank settlement timelines, or technical issues. In such cases, the payment status usually updates automatically after reconciliation.

Customers are encouraged to retain payment confirmation receipts or transaction references until the payment status reflects correctly.

For any clarification regarding EMI amounts, due dates, payment status, or reminders, customers can contact TVS Credit support through official channels.

This policy is intended to provide general information about EMI handling and does not override the terms and conditions specified in the individual loan agreement.
