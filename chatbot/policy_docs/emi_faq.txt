EMI FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS – TVS CREDIT

Q: I have already paid my EMI, why am I still receiving reminders?
A: In some cases, there can be a delay between successful payment and system update due to bank settlement or reconciliation processes. Reminder messages may still be triggered during this period. The payment status usually updates automatically.

Q: My payment was successful but the EMI still shows as due.
A: This can happen if the payment was made close to the due date or outside normal banking hours. Customers are advised to wait for the standard processing time before taking any action.

Q: Should I pay again if the system shows my EMI as pending?
A: No. If you have already completed the payment and received confirmation from your bank or payment provider, do not make a duplicate payment immediately. The system will update once reconciliation is complete.

Q: What should I do if my EMI status does not update after payment?
A: If the EMI status does not update even after the usual processing time, customers should contact TVS Credit support and share the transaction reference or payment confirmation details.

Q: Why am I receiving calls or messages before my EMI due date?
A: Reminder communications may be sent shortly before reminder due date to help customers avoid missing their payment. These are informational reminders.

Q: What happens if I miss my EMI due date?
A: Missing the due date may result in the EMI being marked as overdue. Overdue EMIs can attract late payment charges and may impact the customer’s credit profile as per applicable guidelines.

Q: Can EMI reminder calls or messages stop once I have paid?
A: Once the payment is confirmed and updated in the system, reminder communications are typically stopped automatically.

Q: Is it safe to make EMI payments through links sent by TVS Credit?
A: Customers should only use official payment links or channels shared by TVS Credit. TVS Credit does not ask for confidential details such as OTPs or passwords.

Q: Who should I contact for EMI-related issues?
A: For any EMI-related clarification or issues, customers can reach out to TVS Credit through official customer support channels.
